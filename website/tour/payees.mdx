# Payees

**Payees** lists everyone you pay or get paid by, with how many transactions each one has.

![The Payees page](/img/screenshots/payees.webp)

- Give a payee a **default category**, and new transactions from it get that category
  automatically.
- Rename or delete a payee from its row, and give it a logo by clicking its icon.
- Tick two or more payees to **merge** them into one (handy when a bank spells the same shop
  several ways).

Read [Payees](/docs/payees) in the guide.
