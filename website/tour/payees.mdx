# Payees

Payees are the people and businesses you pay or get paid by. They're created automatically as
you add and import transactions.

- Rename a payee, or give it a logo, and every transaction follows.
- Set a **default category** to fill in the category whenever that payee is used.
- Tick two or more payees and choose **Merge** to combine duplicates, such as `AMAZON.COM` and
  `Amazon`.
- See how many transactions each payee has and search the list.

To tidy payee names automatically as transactions come in, use [Rules](./rules).
