# On your phone

On a [self-hosted server](/community/self-hosting), open FlyBudget in your phone's browser.
Every page has a layout made for small screens: lists turn into cards, dialogs slide up from the
bottom, and every button is big enough to tap.

## Dark mode

Choose **Dark** (or **System**, to follow your device) in **Settings → Preferences**.
