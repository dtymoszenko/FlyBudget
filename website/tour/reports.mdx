# Reports

**Reports** is a set of dashboards, shown as tabs. Each one is a grid of charts: net worth,
income and expenses, spending by category, spending trends, a transaction calendar and any
custom reports you've saved.

<Screenshot name="reports" alt="Report dashboards" />

- **Date range** sets the period for every chart on the dashboard; a chart can also keep its
  own.
- **Edit layout** lets you drag and resize charts, and **Add widget** adds more.
- A chart's ⋮ menu opens its **full view**, with summary figures, a bigger chart and the numbers
  behind it, ready to export as CSV.
- **Custom Report** opens the report builder.

<Screenshot name="report-net-worth" alt="A report's full view" />
