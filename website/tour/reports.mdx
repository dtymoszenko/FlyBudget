# Reports

Reports is a set of dashboards, shown as tabs. Each one is a grid of charts:

- **Date range** at the top sets the period for every chart on the dashboard.
- **Edit layout** lets you drag and resize charts; **Add widget** adds built-in charts or your
  saved custom reports.
- Each chart's ⋮ menu can open its full view, rename it, give it its own date range, move it to
  another dashboard or remove it.
- **Custom Report** opens the report builder.

Read [Reports](/docs/reports) in the guide for the full list of charts.
