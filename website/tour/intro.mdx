# Tour of FlyBudget

Welcome to the FlyBudget tour: a quick look at each screen, so you know your way around before
you start. Every page here is a short overview with a screenshot. When you want the details,
each one links to the matching page of the [user guide](/docs/intro).

You can also click around yourself: [Try the demo](pathname:///demo/) opens the real app in
your browser with a sample budget. Nothing you change is saved or sent anywhere.

![The FlyBudget dashboard](/img/screenshots/dashboard.webp)

## Finding your way around

The **sidebar** on the left is always there:

- The main pages: **Dashboard**, **Accounts**, **Transactions**, **Budget**, **Recurring**,
  **Reports**, **Cash Flow** and **Goals**.
- Your balances: **All accounts** (your net worth), **For budget** (the accounts your budget
  uses) and **Off budget** (investments, property and loans). Open a group to see each account,
  and choose **Add account** to add another.
- At the bottom: **Payees**, **Rules** and **Settings**, and a status line that shows where your
  data lives and whether FlyBudget is connected to it.
- At the top, **?** opens help and the pin keeps the sidebar open or lets it tuck away until you
  point at it.

On a phone the sidebar becomes a menu that slides in from the left (see
[On your phone](./phone)).

## Where to next

Follow the pages in the sidebar in order, or jump to the one you need. If you're setting up
FlyBudget for the first time, [Getting started](/docs/getting-started) walks you through it.
