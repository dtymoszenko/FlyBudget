# Transactions

**Transactions** shows every transaction from every account in one list, grouped by day. Search
by payee or notes, pick a period (this month, the last three months, this year or all time),
and use **Add** to enter a new one.

![All transactions](/img/screenshots/transactions.webp)

Click a transaction to open its details on the right. There you can change the date, category,
payee and notes, see how a split is divided, turn it into a rule with **Create rule**, or delete
it.

![A transaction's details](/img/screenshots/transaction-detail.webp)

Transactions can also be split across categories, moved between your accounts as transfers, and
imported from your bank as a CSV file.

Read [Transactions](/docs/transactions) and [Importing transactions](/docs/importing) in the
guide.
