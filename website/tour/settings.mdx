# Settings

Settings is split into tabs:

- **Categories**: add, rename, reorder and delete categories and groups, and pick their icons.
- **Accounts**: change the order accounts appear in.
- **Connected Banks**: set up SimpleFIN or Plaid and manage your bank connections.
- **Data**: export transactions to CSV, download a full backup, or restore one.
- **Preferences**: theme, sidebar, icons, currency symbol and date format.
- **Server**: where your data lives; on a self-hosted server also a security check, your
  signed-in devices and your password.
