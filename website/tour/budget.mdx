# Budget

The Budget page shows one month at a time. Use the arrows to move between months and **Today**
to come back.

![The Budget page](/img/screenshots/budget.webp)

- Income comes first, then expenses grouped by kind: Fixed, Flexible, Non-Monthly and
  Savings/Investments. Each category shows what you **Planned**, the **Actual** amount and
  what's **Remaining**, with a bar that turns amber and then red as you get close to the plan
  and go over it.
- Click a planned amount to change it, or to use the same amount for the months after.
- The panel on the right shows how much is **Left to budget** and how this month's income and
  spending compare with the plan.
- Click a category's name to see its history and its transactions.

![Overspent categories show in red](/img/screenshots/budget-overspent.webp)

Read [How budgeting works](/docs/budgeting) in the guide to get the most out of it.
