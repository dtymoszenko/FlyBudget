# Budget

The Budget page shows one month at a time. Use the arrows to move between months and
**Today** to come back.

- The table lists income categories, then expense categories grouped by budget type (Fixed,
  Flexible, Non-Monthly and Savings/Investments), each with **Planned**, **Actual** and
  **Remaining** amounts.
- Click a planned amount to change it, or to apply it to the following months as well.
- Categories with nothing planned or spent are tucked away behind **Show inactive categories**.
- The summary on the right shows **To Be Budgeted** and progress bars for income and expenses.

On a phone, groups become collapsible cards and planned amounts open in a sheet.

Read [How budgeting works](/docs/budgeting) to get the most out of it.
