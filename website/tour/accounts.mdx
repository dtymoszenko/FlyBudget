# Accounts

The Accounts page lists every open account, grouped into cash, credit, investments, property
and loans, with its current balance and how it changed over the last month. At the top, a net
worth chart and an assets and liabilities summary show the big picture.

- **Add Account** creates a new account. You can also add one from the sidebar.
- Click an account to open its transactions, where you can **Edit** it, **Reconcile** it, or
  **Update value** for investments, property and loans.

Read [Accounts](/docs/accounts) in the guide to learn about account types, on-budget and
off-budget accounts, and reconciling.
