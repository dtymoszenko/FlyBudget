# Accounts

The Accounts page lists every open account, grouped into cash, credit, investments, property
and loans, with its balance and how it changed over the last month. Your net worth chart and a
breakdown of assets and liabilities sit at the top.

<Screenshot name="accounts" alt="The Accounts page" />

Click an account to open its **register**: its transactions and balance, with buttons to add
or import transactions, **Reconcile** it against a statement, **Edit** it, or **Update value**
for a home, a car or an investment.

<Screenshot name="account-register" alt="An account's register" />

Read [Accounts](/docs/accounts) in the guide for account types, on-budget and off-budget
accounts, and reconciling.
