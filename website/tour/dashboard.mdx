# Dashboard

The dashboard is the first thing you see when you open FlyBudget: a snapshot of your money on
one page, updated as you add transactions and plan your budget.

<Screenshot name="dashboard" alt="The dashboard" />

From top to bottom:

- **Net worth** over the range you pick, with the change since the start.
- **Left to Spend**, your **average monthly income and expenses**, and your **savings rate**
  against a goal you choose.
- **Cash Flow**: income and expenses for the last six months.
- **Spending**: this month's running total next to last month (or another period you pick).
- **Budget**: planned and spent for each kind of spending this month.
- **Upcoming Bills**: what's due in the next 30 days.
- **Spending by Category** and your **Recent Transactions**.

While you're setting up, a **Get started** checklist at the top ticks itself off as you go.

Read [The dashboard](/docs/dashboard) in the guide for what each card shows and how it's
calculated.
