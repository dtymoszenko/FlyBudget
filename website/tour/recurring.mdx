# Recurring

The Recurring page has two tabs.

- **Monthly** shows the bills, subscriptions and income expected this month, as a list or a
  calendar, with how much is paid and how much is left for income and expenses.
- **All recurring** is a searchable table of every recurring item with its payee, account, next
  date, status and amount.

**Add recurring** creates one by hand, and **Find recurring** suggests them from your past
transactions. When a transaction might belong to a recurring item, a suggestion appears at the
top of the page to confirm.

Read [Recurring transactions](/docs/recurring) in the guide for details.
