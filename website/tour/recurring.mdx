# Recurring

**Recurring** keeps track of the bills, subscriptions and paychecks that repeat. It has two tabs.

**Monthly** shows one month: how much income is still to come and how much of your bills is
paid, then each item with its date and status (paid, upcoming, due or missed). Switch between a
**List** and a **Calendar**.

![This month's recurring transactions](/img/screenshots/recurring.webp)

![The calendar view](/img/screenshots/recurring-calendar.webp)

**All recurring** lists everything you track, with its next date, status, amount and how often
it repeats.

Two buttons at the top: **Find recurring** looks through your past transactions for things that
repeat, and **Add recurring** adds one yourself. When a new transaction looks like a payment
you're expecting, a banner asks you to confirm the match.

Read [Recurring transactions](/docs/recurring) in the guide.
