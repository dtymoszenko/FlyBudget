# Cash Flow

**Cash Flow** draws where your money came from and where it went: income sources on the left,
flowing into category groups and then single categories, with what you saved at the top. Above
it are your total income, total expenses, net income and savings rate for the period.

<Screenshot name="cash-flow" alt="The Cash Flow page" />

Pick a period at the top right (one, three or six months, this year, last year or custom
dates), and use **Export CSV** to save the numbers.

Click a spending group or category in the diagram to see its transactions below. The list uses
that category and the chart’s selected date range; choosing another node clears any search in
the previous list. Click **Uncategorized** to see only entries without a category, including
splits with an uncategorized part. Linked transfers and uncategorized balance adjustments are
excluded. Refunds remain in the list because they reduce the chart’s net spending.

You can also Tab to a spending label and press Enter or Space to select it. On a phone, expand
a group and tap a category or **All of …** to open the same filtered list. Use **Clear** above
the transactions to close the list.

Read [Reports](/docs/reports#cash-flow) in the guide.
