# Cash Flow

**Cash Flow** draws where your money came from and where it went: income sources on the left,
flowing into category groups and then single categories, with what you saved at the top. Above
it are your total income, total expenses, net income and savings rate for the period.

<Screenshot name="cash-flow" alt="The Cash Flow page" />

Pick a period at the top right (one, three or six months, this year, last year or custom
dates), and use **Export CSV** to save the numbers.

Read [Reports](/docs/reports#cash-flow) in the guide.
