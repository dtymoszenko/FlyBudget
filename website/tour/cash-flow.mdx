# Cash Flow

Cash Flow draws a diagram of where your money came from and where it went for the period you
choose: income sources on one side, flowing into spending categories and savings on the other.
The wider a band, the more money it carries. Hover over a band to see its amount.

Read [Reports](/docs/reports) in the guide for the other ways to look at your money.
