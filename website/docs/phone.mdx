# Using FlyBudget on your phone

The desktop app runs on one computer. To open your budget on your phone (or any other device),
run FlyBudget on a [self-hosted server](/community/self-hosting) and open its address in your
phone's browser. Sign in with your server's password, and you'll stay signed in on that phone for
30 days.

## Made for small screens

Every page works on a phone:

- The **menu** button at the top left opens the sidebar; tap a page or outside it to close it.
- **Transactions** become cards. Tap one to open its details full screen.
- **Budget** groups become cards you can open and close. Tap a planned amount to change it in a
  sheet that slides up from the bottom. Inactive categories are behind a button.
- **Payees** and **Rules** become cards, with their actions in a ⋮ menu. Rules move with **Move
  up** and **Move down** instead of dragging.
- **Adding a transaction** opens a sheet with one field per row.
- **Report dashboards** show widgets one under another.
- Dialogs open as sheets from the bottom, and every button is big enough to tap.

## Add it to your home screen

In your phone's browser, choose **Add to Home Screen** (Safari: the share button; Chrome: the ⋮
menu). FlyBudget then opens like an app. On a server with HTTPS, the app itself is saved on your
phone, so it opens even when the server can't be reached (see [Working offline](./offline)).

## Sharing a budget

Everyone who knows the server's password can open the same budget from their own devices, at the
same time. There are no separate user accounts yet: everyone sees and can change everything.
Settings → Server lists every signed-in device, and you can sign any of them out.
