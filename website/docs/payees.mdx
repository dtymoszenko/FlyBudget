# Payees

A payee is anyone you pay or get paid by: a shop, your landlord, your employer. FlyBudget creates
payees as you add, import and sync transactions, and **Payees** lists them all with how many
transactions each one has. Search the list with the box at the top.

![The Payees page](/img/screenshots/payees.webp)

## Default categories

Pick a **Default category** for a payee, and new transactions from it are put in that category
when nothing else decides: a category you choose yourself comes first, then your
[rules](./rules), then the payee's default. It's the quickest way to categorize the shops you
visit every week.

**Run rules** on the Rules page (with every rule) also fills in default categories for past
transactions that are still uncategorized.

## Renaming and deleting

Double-click a payee's name to rename it (Enter saves, Escape cancels), and use the trash button
at the end of its row to delete it. On a phone, both are in the row's ⋮ menu. Renaming changes
the name on all of its transactions. Deleting a payee keeps its transactions; they just lose the
link to it.

## Logos

Click a payee's icon to give it a logo (a PNG, JPEG or WebP image); the × removes it. Logos also
show on transactions and in the transaction details panel, where you can change them too. Turn
merchant icons off in **Settings → Preferences → Icons**.

## Merging payees

Banks often spell the same place several ways (`AMZN MKTP`, `Amazon.com`, `AMAZON PRIME`). Tick
two or more payees, choose **Merge**, and pick the name to keep. All of their transactions move
to that payee, and a logo is kept if the payee you keep doesn't have one.

To stop new variations from appearing, add a [rule](./rules) that renames them: "if imported
description contains `AMZN` → rename payee to **Amazon**".
