# How budgeting works

FlyBudget uses **zero-based budgeting**: at the start of each month you give every dollar you
have a job, until there's nothing left to assign.

## The Budget page

Each row is a category, grouped into income and expenses:

- **Planned**: how much you expect to earn or spend this month. Click (or tap) an amount to
  change it. You can also apply the same amount to the months after.
- **Actual**: what came in or went out, from your on-budget accounts.
- **Remaining**: what's left to spend. Unspent money carries over to next month; overspending
  carries over too, so it's covered by next month's plan.

Expense categories are organized by budget type (Fixed, Flexible, Non-Monthly and
Savings/Investments), so you can see at a glance which spending you control.

## To Be Budgeted

```text
To Be Budgeted = income received + carry-over from last month − total planned
```

- **Positive**: you have money without a job yet. Plan it into a category, or into savings.
- **Zero**: every dollar has a job. That's the goal.
- **Negative**: you've planned more than you have. Lower a category until it's back to zero.

Only on-budget accounts count. Investments, property and loans appear in your net worth but
not here (see [Accounts](./accounts)).

## Categories

Add, rename, reorder and give emoji icons to categories and groups in **Settings →
Categories**. Deleting a category asks where its transactions should move.

## A simple routine

1. When you get paid, plan that income into your categories until To Be Budgeted is zero.
2. As you spend, keep an eye on **Remaining**.
3. If a category runs out, move money from one that has some left.

See the [Budget tour](/tour/budget) for every control on the page.
