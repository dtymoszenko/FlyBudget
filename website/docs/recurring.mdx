# Recurring transactions

Recurring transactions are the bills, subscriptions and paychecks that repeat. FlyBudget shows
what's due, what's paid, and what's coming up.

## Adding one

On **Recurring**, choose **Add recurring** and fill in the name, payee, account, amount and how
often it repeats: weekly, every two weeks, twice a month, monthly, quarterly, twice a year or
yearly. The amount can be exact, approximate or variable, which helps FlyBudget match it.

Tick **Auto-create transactions** and FlyBudget adds the transaction for you on its due date.

## Find recurring

Already have a few months of transactions? **Find recurring** looks for payees that repeat on a
regular schedule with similar amounts and suggests them. Tick the ones you want and they're
created, already linked to the past payments.

## Paid, upcoming and missed

When a transaction arrives that matches a recurring item (same payee, close to the expected
date and amount), FlyBudget links them and marks it paid. Suggestions it isn't sure about
appear at the top of the page for you to confirm.

- **Monthly** shows this month's items as a list or a calendar, with totals for income and
  expenses.
- **All recurring** lists everything with its next date and status: missed, due, upcoming or
  scheduled. How far ahead counts as "upcoming" is up to you.

Upcoming bills also appear on the dashboard.
