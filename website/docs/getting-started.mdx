# Getting started

The first time you open FlyBudget, it asks how you'd like to add your accounts. Once you're in,
the dashboard shows a **Get started with FlyBudget** checklist that ticks itself off as you go.
These are the same steps, in a bit more detail.

## 1. Add your accounts

An account is anything that holds money or debt: a checking account, a credit card, cash, a
loan, an investment account or your home.

- **Connect with Plaid** (free developer account; Plaid approves access to real banks) or
  **Connect with SimpleFIN** ($1.50 per month): import transactions automatically. See [Connecting a bank](./bank-sync).
- **Add accounts manually**: enter a name, pick the type and type today's balance yourself.
  Transactions are manual too: download CSV files from your bank and import them yourself, or
  enter each one by hand. Nothing updates on its own.

Checking, savings, cash and credit card accounts count toward your budget. Investments,
property and loans are tracked for [net worth](./reports) but stay off budget.
[More about accounts](./accounts).

:::tip
You can skip this step and look around first. Choose **Skip for now and look around** on the
welcome screen; the checklist on the dashboard reminds you where you left off.
:::

## 2. Bring in your transactions

Open an account and choose **Import CSV** to load a file downloaded from your bank's website,
or **Add Transaction** to enter one by hand. FlyBudget spots rows you've already imported, so
importing an overlapping file twice is safe. [More about transactions](./transactions).

## 3. Plan this month's budget

Open **Budget** and type an amount in the **Planned** column for each category you expect to
spend on (and for your expected income). **To Be Budgeted** shows how much money still needs a
job. [How budgeting works](./budgeting).

## 4. Add your bills and paychecks

On **Recurring**, add the things that repeat, like rent, subscriptions and your salary. Or
choose **Find recurring** and FlyBudget suggests them from the transactions you already have.
[More about recurring transactions](./recurring).

## 5. Categorize automatically

Rules sort new transactions for you: "if the payee contains _Netflix_, set the category to
_Streaming_". Create them on **Rules**, or from any transaction with **Create rule**.
[More about rules](./rules).

## Keep a backup

Your budget lives on your computer (or your server), so backing it up is up to you. Settings →
Data → **Download Backup** saves everything in one file. [More about backups](./backups).
