# Transactions

## Adding a transaction

- **All Transactions → Add** opens a form where you pick the account.
- On an **account page**, **Add Transaction** adds a row at the top of the list (on a phone it
  opens as a sheet).

The payee field suggests people and businesses you've used before. If a payee has a default
category, it's filled in for you, and your [rules](./rules) run as soon as you save.

## Importing a CSV file

Most banks let you download your transactions as a CSV file. Open the account, choose
**Import CSV**, pick the file, and tell FlyBudget what each column holds: **Date**, **Payee**,
**Amount** (or separate **Inflow** and **Outflow** columns), **Notes**, or **Skip**. The preview
marks rows you already have as duplicates, so you can import overlapping date ranges safely.

## Splits

One purchase can cover several categories, like a supermarket trip that's part groceries and
part household. Open the transaction and split it into parts; the parts always add up to the
total.

## Transfers

Moving money between two of your accounts (paying a credit card from checking, for example) is
a transfer, not spending. Transfers are linked: change the amount or date on one side and the
other follows; delete one side and the other becomes a normal transaction.

## Working offline

On a self-hosted server, if the connection drops, transactions you add are kept on your device
and sent in order when it comes back. Edits and deletes wait for the connection, so you never
overwrite a change made on another device.
