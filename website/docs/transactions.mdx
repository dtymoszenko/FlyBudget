# Transactions

Every purchase, paycheck and payment is a transaction. You can see them all together on
**Transactions**, or one account at a time on its register (click the account in the sidebar or
on the Accounts page).

<Screenshot name="transactions" alt="All transactions" />

## The list

Transactions are grouped by day, newest first, with each day's total on its header and a count
of what matches your filters above the list. Each row shows:

- the **payee**, with its logo or initial (transfers show two arrows),
- the **category** (a split shows **Split (2)** and can be expanded; a transfer shows
  **Transfer**),
- the **account** (on the all-transactions list only),
- a lock if it's [reconciled](./accounts#reconciling),
- the **amount**: money coming in is green.

## Finding transactions

- **Search** matches payee names and notes as you type.
- **This Month**, **Last 3 Months**, **This Year** and **All Time** pick the period.
- Hover over a category and click the arrow next to it to show only that category (the period
  switches to All Time so nothing is missed). The filter appears above the list; remove it with
  its ×. The same arrow next to a payee searches for all of that payee's transactions.

## Adding a transaction

**From Transactions**: choose **Add**. Pick **Debit** (money out) or **Credit** (money in), then
enter the amount, the merchant, the date and the account; category and notes are optional. The
merchant box suggests payees you've used before, or creates a new one.

<Screenshot name="transaction-add" alt="The Add transaction dialog" dialog />

**From an account's register**: choose **Add Transaction** and fill in the row that appears at
the top: date, payee, category, notes, and an **Outflow** or **Inflow** amount. Save with the
check mark, or cancel with the ×. On a phone the form opens as a sheet, one field per row.

<Screenshot name="transaction-add-inline" alt="Adding a transaction in the register" />

When you save, FlyBudget fills in what it can: if the payee has a
[default category](./payees#default-categories) and you didn't pick one, it's used, and your
[rules](./rules) run on the new transaction. Anything you chose yourself is kept.

## Editing a transaction

Click a transaction to open its details on the right (full screen on a phone). Change the
**date**, **category**, **payee** or **notes**; each change is saved when you leave the field.
The panel also shows the [recurring item](./recurring) it paid, if any, with a button to unlink
it.

<Screenshot name="transaction-detail" alt="A transaction's details" />

For quick fixes you don't need the panel: hover over the payee or category in the list and click
it to pick another. The category picker can also create a new category on the spot.

- **Create rule** (in the panel) starts a [rule](./rules) from this transaction, with its payee
  and category filled in.
- **Delete Transaction** removes it, after asking. Deleting a transfer leaves the other side as
  a normal transaction; deleting a split removes its parts; deleting a payment for a recurring
  item marks that payment as unpaid again.

Reconciled transactions are locked: the panel says so, and the date, category, payee and notes
can't be changed.

## Splits

One purchase can cover several categories, like a warehouse-store trip that's part groceries and
part home goods. When adding a transaction in a register, click the split button (next to the
check mark): the category becomes **Split** and rows appear for each part, with its own
category, notes and amount. Add more with **Add split**. The form shows whether the parts add up
to the total (**Balanced**) or how much is left.

In the list, a split shows **Split (N)**; click the arrow to see its parts. The detail panel
shows each part with its category and amount.

<Screenshot name="transaction-split" alt="A split transaction" />

Budgets and reports count each part in its own category. The account balance counts the
transaction once. A [rule](./rules) can also split transactions for you.

## Transfers

Moving money between your own accounts (paying a credit card from checking, or saving into a
savings account) is a transfer, not spending. In a register, pick
**Transfer: _account name_** at the bottom of the category list. FlyBudget creates two linked
transactions: money out of this account and into the other.

Transfers are linked: change the date or amount on one side and the other follows. They have no
category, and they're left out of income and spending.

## Importing and syncing

- [Import a CSV file](./importing) from your bank.
- [Connect a bank](./bank-sync) with SimpleFIN or Plaid to bring transactions in
  automatically.

## When the connection drops

On a [self-hosted server](./phone), transactions you add while FlyBudget can't reach the
server are kept on your device and sent when it's back. See
[Working offline](./offline).
