# Accounts

An account is anything that holds money or debt: a checking account, a credit card, cash in
your wallet, a loan, a retirement account or your home. Every transaction belongs to one
account.

![The Accounts page](/img/screenshots/accounts.webp)

The **Accounts** page shows your net worth over time, your assets and liabilities by kind, and
every open account grouped by type, with its balance and how that group changed over the last
month. The sidebar shows the same balances: **All accounts** is your net worth, **For budget**
adds up the accounts your budget uses, and **Off budget** the rest.

## Adding an account

Choose **Add Account** (on the Accounts page, or **Add account** in the sidebar), then enter:

- **Account Name**, like "Chase Checking".
- **Account Type** (see below).
- **Current Balance** as of today. For a credit card or loan, enter what you owe as a positive
  number; FlyBudget records it as a debt.
- **Off budget**, ticked for you on investments, property and loans (see below).

<img

  alt="The Add Account dialog"
/>

To create accounts from your bank instead, [connect a bank](./bank-sync): FlyBudget adds its
accounts for you, or links them to accounts you already have.

## Account types

| Group       | Types                                         | On budget by default |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Cash        | Checking, savings, cash                       | Yes                  |
| Credit      | Credit card, line of credit                   | Yes                  |
| Investments | Investment, retirement, crypto                | No                   |
| Property    | Real estate, vehicle, valuables               | No                   |
| Loans       | Mortgage, auto loan, student loan, other loan | No                   |
| Other       | Other asset, other liability                  | No                   |

Credit cards, lines of credit, loans and other liabilities are **debts**: their balances are
negative and count against your net worth.

## On budget and off budget

Money in **on-budget** accounts is money you plan with: its income counts toward
[To Be Budgeted](./budgeting#to-be-budgeted) and its spending shows up in your categories.
**Off-budget** accounts (investments, property, loans) still count toward your net worth, but
their transactions stay out of the budget. Change it for any account with the **Off budget**
box in its **Edit** dialog.

## An account's register

Click an account to open its register: its balance, type and every transaction in it. From the
header you can:

- **Add Transaction** or **Import CSV** (see [Transactions](./transactions) and
  [Importing transactions](./importing)).
- **Update value** (or **Update balance** for a debt) for investments, property and loans.
- **Reconcile** it against a bank statement.
- **Edit** it.

![An account's register](/img/screenshots/account-register.webp)

## Updating the value of an investment or property

Homes, cars and investment accounts usually don't have transactions worth entering one by one.
Choose **Update value**, enter what it's worth today, and FlyBudget adds a dated adjustment for
the difference, so your net worth history stays right.

<img
  className="screenshot-dialog"
  src="/img/screenshots/update-value.webp"
  alt="Updating the value of a car"
/>

Adjustments (from **Update value** or from reconciling) change the balance and your net worth,
but they aren't money earned or spent, so income and spending reports leave them out. If you
give one a category, it counts like any other transaction.

## Reconciling

Reconciling checks that FlyBudget agrees with your bank. Do it when a statement arrives, or
whenever the balances look different.

1. Open the account and choose **Reconcile**.
2. Enter the **Statement Balance** from your statement or online banking, and choose
   **Start Reconciliation**.
3. Tick each transaction that appears on the statement. FlyBudget shows the balance of what
   you've ticked and the **Difference** from the statement.
4. When the difference is zero, choose **Finish**. If you can't find what's missing, choose
   **Create Adjustment** instead: FlyBudget adds a reconciliation adjustment for the difference.

![Reconciling an account](/img/screenshots/reconcile.webp)

Reconciled transactions get a lock icon and can't be edited or deleted, so rules and bank sync
never change them.

## Editing and closing an account

**Edit** changes the name, type, starting balance and whether it's off budget, and lets you add
a **logo** (a PNG, JPEG or WebP image, cropped to a square) in place of the colored initials.
You can hide account logos everywhere in **Settings → Preferences → Icons**.

**Close Account** (at the bottom of the Edit dialog) takes it off the sidebar and the Accounts
page. Its transactions stay, so past reports and your net worth history remain correct.

To change the order accounts appear in, use **Settings → Accounts**.
