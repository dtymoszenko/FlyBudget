# Accounts

Every transaction belongs to an account. Add one from **Accounts → Add Account**, or connect a
bank to create them for you.

## Account types

| Group       | Types                                         | On budget by default |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Cash        | Checking, savings, cash                       | Yes                  |
| Credit      | Credit card, line of credit                   | Yes                  |
| Investments | Investment, retirement, crypto                | No                   |
| Property    | Real estate, vehicle, valuables               | No                   |
| Loans       | Mortgage, auto loan, student loan, other loan | No                   |
| Other       | Other asset, other liability                  | No                   |

Credit cards, lines of credit and loans are **liabilities**: their balances are negative and
count against your net worth.

## On budget and off budget

Money in **on-budget** accounts is money you can plan with: it counts toward _To Be Budgeted_
and category spending. **Off-budget** accounts (investments, property, loans) still count
toward your net worth but stay out of the budget. You can change this for any account in its
settings.

## Updating the value of an investment or property

Homes, cars and investment accounts usually don't have transactions worth tracking one by one.
Open the account and choose **Update value** (or **Update balance** for a loan): FlyBudget adds
a dated adjustment so your net worth history stays accurate.

Adjustments (from **Update value** or from reconciling) change the balance and your net worth,
but they aren't money earned or spent, so income and spending reports leave them out. If you
give one a category, it counts like any other transaction.

## Reconciling

Reconciling checks that FlyBudget agrees with your bank statement. Open an account, choose
**Reconcile**, enter the statement's ending balance, and tick the transactions that appear on
the statement until the difference is zero. Reconciled transactions are locked, so rules and
bank sync never change them.

## Closing an account

Close an account from its **Edit** dialog. It disappears from the sidebar and the account list,
but its transactions stay, so past reports remain correct.
