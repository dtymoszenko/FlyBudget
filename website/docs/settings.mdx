# Settings

**Settings** is split into tabs. The tab you're on is part of the page's address, so you can
bookmark one.

<Screenshot name="settings" alt="Settings: Categories" />

## Categories

Your income and expense categories, in their groups. **Create group** adds a group for income or
expenses, and **Create Category** adds a category to a group. **Edit** a category to change its
icon, name, group and budget type; drag categories and groups to reorder them; **Delete** a
category and choose where its transactions move. See [How budgeting works](./budgeting#categories).

## Accounts

**Account Order**: drag accounts into the order you want them in the sidebar and on the Accounts
page.

## Connected Banks

Set up [SimpleFIN or Plaid](./bank-sync), see each connected bank with when it last synced,
**Sync Now**, and disconnect.

## Data

- **Export Transactions**: save transactions as a CSV file for a spreadsheet, optionally only
  those between two dates.
- **Full Backup**: **Download Backup** saves everything in one file.
- **Restore from Backup**: replaces your budget with a backup file.

See [Backups and exports](./backups).

## Preferences

<Screenshot name="settings-preferences" alt="Settings: Preferences" />

| Setting             | Choices                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Theme**           | Light, Dark, or System (follows your computer or phone).                                                                            |
| **Sidebar**         | **Pinned** (always open) or **Auto-hide** (collapsed, opens when you point at it). The pin at the top of the sidebar does the same. |
| **Icons**           | Show or hide merchant, category and account icons.                                                                                  |
| **Currency Symbol** | Up to 3 characters, like `$`, `€`, `£` or `EUR`. It only changes how amounts are shown, not their value.                            |
| **Date Format**     | `Jan 5, 2026`, `01/05/2026`, `05/01/2026` or `2026-01-05`.                                                                          |

<Screenshot name="dark-budget" alt="The budget in dark mode" />

**Reset to defaults** puts them all back. Preferences are saved in your browser (or in the
desktop app) rather than in your budget, so each device can have its own.

## Server

**Where your data lives**, and the version of FlyBudget you're running.

- **Desktop app**: your budget is a file on this computer. This tab links to the
  [self-hosting guide](/community/self-hosting) if you'd like to use FlyBudget on other devices.
- **Self-hosted server**: a **Security check** of your setup (HTTPS, proxy, allowed hosts,
  encryption key, password) with a hint for anything to fix, your **Signed-in devices** (sign out
  any of them, or all the others), **Change password**, **Sign out**, and
  **Keep a copy of my budget on this device** (see [Working offline](./offline)).

## Undo

Made a mistake? Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> on a Mac) to undo
your last change, and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> to redo it. A message at the
bottom of the screen says what was undone, with a button to redo it. Undo works for changes to
transactions, accounts, budget amounts, categories, payees, rules, recurring items, goals,
reports and dashboards, while you're not typing in a field.

The status line at the bottom of the sidebar shows where your budget is stored and whether
FlyBudget can reach it; click it for details, including the version.
