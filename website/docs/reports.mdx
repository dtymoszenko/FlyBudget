# Reports

FlyBudget has three places to look at your money over time: **report dashboards**, the
**custom report builder**, and **Cash Flow**.

## Dashboards

**Reports** is a set of dashboards, shown as tabs. Each one is a grid of charts (widgets). A new
budget starts with an **Overview** dashboard; add more with **+** next to the tabs, and rename or
delete them from the ⋮ menu on the right (**Rename dashboard…**, **Delete dashboard**; the last
dashboard can't be deleted).

![Report dashboards](/img/screenshots/reports.webp)

### Widgets

Choose **Add widget** to add one of these, or any custom report you've saved:

| Widget                   | What it shows                                                                                      |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Summary**              | Total income, total expenses, average monthly expenses and average per transaction for the period. |
| **Net Worth**            | Net worth over time, with assets and liabilities.                                                  |
| **Income & Expenses**    | Income and expenses for each month.                                                                |
| **Spending by Category** | Your spending categories, biggest first.                                                           |
| **Spending Trends**      | How spending in up to five categories changed over time (your top five, unless you pick them).     |
| **Transaction Calendar** | Money in and out on each day.                                                                      |

Transfers between your accounts and balance adjustments aren't income or spending, so reports
leave them out. Split transactions count each part in its own category.

### Arranging a dashboard

Choose **Edit layout** to drag widgets around and resize them from their corners; choose
**Done** when you're finished. Layouts are saved as you go. On a phone, widgets are shown one
under another.

![Editing a dashboard's layout](/img/screenshots/reports-edit-layout.webp)

Each widget's ⋮ menu can **Open** its full view (**Edit report** for a custom report),
**Rename** it, give it its own **Date range**, **Freeze dates** or **Unfreeze**, **Move to**
another dashboard, or **Remove from dashboard**. Removed a widget by mistake? Press

<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> on a Mac) to [undo](./settings#undo).

### Date ranges

The **Date range** bar at the top of a dashboard sets the period for all of its widgets: 1M, 3M,
6M, 12M, year to date, last year or all. These ranges are **live**: "last 6 months" moves forward
as time passes. **Frozen** keeps exact months instead.

A widget can keep its own range; the bar then says how many widgets do, and the widget's title
shows a pin. Choose **Use dashboard range** in its full view to go back to the dashboard's.

Short ranges show more detail: Net Worth draws a point for each day for ranges up to three
months, and Spending Trends shows a running total by day for a single month.

### Full view

Open a widget for its full view: four summary figures, a larger chart, and a table of the
numbers behind it. **Export CSV** saves that table. Change the range here and choose **Save to
widget** to keep it; for Spending Trends you can also pick which categories to show.

![The Net Worth report](/img/screenshots/report-net-worth.webp)

The **Transaction Calendar** shows each day of up to three months as a square with a green bar
for money in and a red bar for money out. Longer ranges become a heatmap, one small square per
day, colored by whether more came in or went out. Click a day to list its transactions.

![The Transaction Calendar](/img/screenshots/report-calendar.webp)

## Custom reports

**Custom Report** opens the report builder. Choose:

- **Chart Type**: bar, stacked bar, line, area, donut or table.
- **Mode**: **Total** for the whole period, or **Over Time** for each month.
- **Group By**: category, category group, payee, account or month.
- **Balance Type**: expenses, income or net.
- **Date Range**: 1M, 3M, 6M, 12M, year to date, last year, all, or custom dates.
- **Accounts** and **Categories** to include.

![The custom report builder](/img/screenshots/custom-report.webp)

The chart updates as you change the settings. **Save** it to reopen later from the list at the
top; saving adds it to a dashboard as a widget, and **Update** saves changes to a saved report.
**Export** downloads the numbers as CSV.

## Cash Flow

**Cash Flow** draws where your money came from and where it went for a period: income sources on
the left, flowing into category groups and then single categories, with what you saved on top.
Above it are your total income, total expenses, net income and savings rate.

![The Cash Flow page](/img/screenshots/cash-flow.webp)

Pick the period at the top (1M, 3M, 6M, this year, last year or custom), and use **Export CSV**
to save the numbers.
