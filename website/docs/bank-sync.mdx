# Connecting a bank

FlyBudget can import transactions automatically through two services. Both are optional: CSV
import works with almost every bank and costs nothing.

|              | Plaid                                                                       | SimpleFIN Bridge                                                |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Cost**     | Free Plaid developer account                                                | $1.50 per month, paid to SimpleFIN                              |
| **Setup**    | Sign up for a Plaid developer account, then enter your client ID and secret | Create an account at SimpleFIN Bridge, then paste a setup token |
| **Best for** | People comfortable with developer dashboards                                | Most people                                                     |

Connect either one from **Settings → Connected Banks**, or from the welcome screen.

## Plaid

1. Create a free developer account at [Plaid](https://dashboard.plaid.com/signup) and copy your
   **client ID** and **secret**.
2. Enter them in **Settings → Connected Banks**. Use **Sandbox** to try it with test banks, or
   **Production** for your real accounts.
3. Choose **Connect Bank**. Plaid's sign-in opens in your web browser, which is how banks
   that use their own login page (like Chase) are supported.
4. Choose which FlyBudget account each bank account belongs to.

Disconnecting a bank revokes its access at Plaid before FlyBudget forgets it.

## SimpleFIN

1. Sign up at [SimpleFIN Bridge](https://bridge.simplefin.org) and connect your banks there.
2. Create a **setup token** and paste it into FlyBudget.
3. Choose which FlyBudget account each bank account belongs to.

FlyBudget exchanges the one-time token for a private access link, stored encrypted. To stop
syncing completely, disconnect in FlyBudget _and_ remove the app in SimpleFIN Bridge.

## How syncing works

- New transactions are imported when FlyBudget starts and whenever you choose **Sync Now**.
- Your [rules](./rules) run on every imported transaction.
- Reconciled transactions are never changed by a sync.
- Account balances are adjusted to match what your bank reports.

## Your credentials

Bank tokens never leave the FlyBudget server, and they are encrypted at rest. They are never
included in [backups](./backups). See the [security overview](/community/security) for details.
