# Connecting a bank

FlyBudget can import transactions automatically through three services. All are optional:
[importing files](./importing) (CSV, or CAMT.053 statements) works with almost every bank and
costs nothing.

|              | Plaid                                                                                  | SimpleFIN Bridge                                                | Enable Banking                                                             |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Banks**    | US and Canada                                                                          | Mostly US                                                       | Europe (EEA)                                                               |
| **Cost**     | Free Plaid developer account                                                           | $1.50 per month, paid to SimpleFIN                              | Free for your own accounts                                                 |
| **Setup**    | Sign up for a Plaid developer account, request Production access, then enter your keys | Create an account at SimpleFIN Bridge, then paste a setup token | Create an Enable Banking application, link your accounts, then add its key |
| **Best for** | People comfortable with developer dashboards                                           | Most people in the US                                           | Most people in Europe                                                      |

Connect any of them from **Settings → Connected Banks**, the **+** next to Accounts in the
sidebar, or the welcome screen.

## Plaid

Plaid gives you two sets of API keys:

- **Sandbox** keys work as soon as you sign up, but only with Plaid's test banks and made-up
  transactions. Use them to try FlyBudget's bank sync.
- **Production** keys connect your real banks. They aren't available at signup: you request
  Production access in the Plaid dashboard, answering a few questions about how you'll use it,
  and Plaid reviews the request. That can take anywhere from minutes to a few days.

Plaid's plans and limits for personal use change from time to time; check
[Plaid's pricing](https://plaid.com/pricing/) for what applies to you.

1. Create a free developer account at [Plaid](https://dashboard.plaid.com/signup).
2. Request **Production** access in the Plaid dashboard, and wait for Plaid to approve it.
3. Copy your **client ID** and your Production **secret**, and enter them in **Settings →
   Connected Banks** with the environment set to **Production** (or use your Sandbox secret with
   **Sandbox** to try it out first).
4. Choose **Connect Bank**. Plaid's sign-in opens in your web browser, which is how banks
   that use their own login page (like Chase) are supported.
5. Choose which FlyBudget account each bank account belongs to.

Disconnecting a bank revokes its access at Plaid before FlyBudget forgets it.

## SimpleFIN

1. Sign up at [SimpleFIN Bridge](https://bridge.simplefin.org) and connect your banks there.
2. Create a **setup token** and paste it into FlyBudget.
3. Choose which FlyBudget account each bank account belongs to.

FlyBudget exchanges the one-time token for a private access link, stored encrypted. To stop
syncing completely, disconnect in FlyBudget _and_ remove the app in SimpleFIN Bridge.

## Enable Banking

[Enable Banking](https://enablebanking.com) connects to banks across the European Economic Area
through PSD2, the EU rules that let you share your own bank data with apps you choose. You use
your own Enable Banking application, which is free for linking your own accounts, so there's no
FlyBudget service in the middle: it's just you, Enable Banking and your bank.

### Setting it up

1. Sign up at the [Enable Banking Control Panel](https://enablebanking.com/cp/applications) and
   add a new application.
2. Choose **Production** (Sandbox only reaches Enable Banking's test banks). Give it any name and
   description; Enable Banking shows the name when you log in at your bank.
3. Under the allowed redirect URLs, add the address FlyBudget shows in **Settings → Connected
   Banks → Enable Banking**:
   - In the **desktop app**: `https://flybudget.org/bank-callback/` (see
     [why](#the-redirect-url) below).
   - On a **self-hosted server**: your server's own address, like
     `https://budget.example.com/api/enable-banking/callback`. It must be **https**.
4. Create the application. Your browser downloads a **.pem** file: that's the application's
   private key, and Enable Banking can't show it again, so keep it.
5. Choose **Activate by linking accounts** and log in at your bank. Free applications can only
   read the accounts you link here, so link every account you want in FlyBudget. If you skip
   this, FlyBudget's list of banks stays empty.
6. In FlyBudget, paste the **application ID** and choose the .pem file. FlyBudget checks them with
   Enable Banking before saving them.

### Connecting a bank

1. Choose **Connect Bank**, your country and your bank, then **Log in at your bank**.
2. Your bank's login opens in your web browser. Log in and confirm there; your bank sends you
   back and FlyBudget carries on by itself.
3. Choose where each of the bank's accounts goes. An account you've already imported
   [CAMT statements](./importing) into is picked for you, by its IBAN.

When you link a bank account to a FlyBudget account that already has transactions, FlyBudget
starts a week before the latest one and recognises transactions you already imported from a file
or typed in (the same amount dated at most 3 days apart, or the same bank reference as a CAMT
statement), so nothing comes in twice. Tick **Also bring in older transactions** to fetch
everything the bank has; the same check applies. A new account gets everything the bank has.

### Good to know

- **Only booked transactions** are imported. Pending ones can change or disappear, and some banks
  give them a new ID once they're booked, which would import them twice.
- **Your bank asks again every 90 to 180 days.** That's PSD2: the access you give ends, and you
  confirm it at your bank again. FlyBudget shows when it ends and warns a week before; choose
  **Reconnect**, log in at your bank, and syncing carries on with the same accounts and history.
- **Banks allow only a few automatic updates a day** (usually four). FlyBudget syncs when it
  starts, unless it synced in the last 6 hours, and whenever you choose **Sync now**; updates you
  start yourself don't count against that limit.
- **The bank account's currency.** A new account gets the bank account's currency (see
  [Currencies](./currencies)). Transactions the bank lists in another currency than the bank
  account itself are left out, and the sync says how many.
- Transactions a bank sends without a date or a readable amount are left out and counted, never
  guessed.

### The redirect URL

After you log in, your bank sends your browser back to the redirect URL with a one-time code.
Enable Banking only accepts https addresses, and the desktop app runs on your computer at
`http://localhost`, which can't have one. So the desktop app uses a small page on flybudget.org
that passes the code on to FlyBudget on your computer (`http://127.0.0.1`). The page only ever
sends you to your own computer and keeps nothing, and the code is useless to anyone without the
private key, which never leaves FlyBudget. Its
[source](https://github.com/dtymoszenko/FlyBudget/tree/main/website/static/bank-callback) is a
few lines.

If the page can't reach FlyBudget (FlyBudget was closed, or your browser blocks it), it shows the
address instead: choose **Logged in, but FlyBudget didn't continue?** in FlyBudget and paste it.
That also works on a self-hosted server without https.

### If something goes wrong

| What FlyBudget says                                    | What to do                                                                        |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| No banks to choose from                                | Link your accounts in the Enable Banking Control Panel (step 5), then try again.  |
| The redirect URL isn't registered in your application  | Add the exact address FlyBudget shows to the application's allowed redirect URLs. |
| Enable Banking didn't accept the application ID or key | Check the application ID, and choose the .pem file of that same application.      |
| Your bank allows only a few updates a day              | Wait until tomorrow, or choose **Sync now** yourself.                             |
| Your bank access has ended                             | Choose **Reconnect** and log in at your bank again.                               |

Disconnecting a bank ends its access at Enable Banking before FlyBudget forgets it. If Enable
Banking can't be reached, the connection is kept so you can try again. If you connected the same
bank twice, disconnecting one only removes it from FlyBudget and its access ends on its own date:
banks end the access for all your connections to them at once, which would stop the other one too.

## How syncing works

- New transactions are imported when FlyBudget starts and whenever you choose **Sync Now**
  (for Enable Banking, at most every 6 hours on start; see above).
- Your [rules](./rules) run on every imported transaction.
- Reconciled transactions are never changed by a sync.
- Account balances are adjusted to match what your bank reports.

## Your credentials

Bank tokens never leave the FlyBudget server, and they are encrypted at rest. They are never
included in [backups](./backups). See the [security overview](/community/security) for details.
