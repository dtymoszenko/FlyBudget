# Goals

Goals track what you're saving toward: an emergency fund, a trip, a down payment or a new
laptop.

![The Goals page](/img/screenshots/goals.webp)

The cards at the top add up how much you've saved across all your goals (and what share of the
total that is), your combined targets, what's left to save, and how many goals you've reached.
Below, goals are listed **In progress** and **Completed**.

## Adding a goal

Choose **Add Goal** and enter:

- **Name** and **Target** amount.
- **Saved so far**, if you've already started.
- A **Target date** (optional). FlyBudget works out how much to set aside each month to reach the
  goal on time, and shows it under the progress bar ("$170/mo to reach it on time").
- A **linked account** (optional). The goal's progress then follows that account's balance, so it
  updates by itself as you save. Useful for a savings account you keep for one purpose.
- An **Icon** and **Color** to tell your goals apart at a glance.

<img className="screenshot-dialog" src="/img/screenshots/goal-add.webp" alt="The Add Goal dialog" />

## Updating progress

For a goal without a linked account, edit it from its ⋮ menu and change **Saved so far** as you
save. When you reach the target, the goal moves to **Completed** and says **Goal reached**.

## Goals and your budget

Goals don't move money on their own. To save toward one each month, plan the amount in a savings
category on the [Budget](./budgeting) (the demo budget has a **Savings Goals** group for this),
and transfer it to the savings account the goal follows.
